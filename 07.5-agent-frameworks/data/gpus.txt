GPU notes

RTX 5070 (my PC): 12 GB of GDDR7 memory, about 672 GB/s of memory bandwidth.
Tesla T4 (free Colab): 16 GB of GDDR6 memory, about 320 GB/s of memory bandwidth.
Generating one token means reading every weight once, so the most tokens per second
a GPU can generate is roughly its memory bandwidth divided by the model's size.
